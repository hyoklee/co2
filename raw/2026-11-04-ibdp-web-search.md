# Web search notes: Illinois Basin – Decatur Project (IBDP) literature

Clipped 2026-11-04 from web searches. These notes come from abstracts, bibliographic
records, news items and DOE/NETL slides; no full-text paper was read.

## Project overview

- Greenberg, S. & Finley, R. J., "An overview of the Illinois Basin – Decatur Project",
  *Greenhouse Gases: Science and Technology* 4(5): 571–579, 2014. DOI 10.1002/ghg.1433.
  - https://ideas.repec.org/a/wly/greenh/v4y2014i5p571-579.html
  - Partners: the Midwest Geological Sequestration Consortium (MGSC), led by the Illinois
    State Geological Survey (ISGS), with Schlumberger Carbon Services and ADM (Decatur, IL).
  - Injected about 1 million tonnes of supercritical CO2 over three years into the base of
    the Mt. Simon Sandstone, a saline sandstone about 500 m (1,640 ft) thick.
  - The same issue has companion papers on the seismic characterization and CO2
    monitoring programme (pp. 626–644) and on risk management (pp. 604–616).
- NETL fact sheet, "Illinois Basin Decatur Project":
  https://netl.doe.gov/sites/default/files/2017-11/Illinois-Basin-Decatur-Project.pdf
  - Operational injection ran from November 2011 to November 2014.

## Induced seismicity

- Luu et al., "Coupled Hydromechanical Modeling of Induced Seismicity From CO2 Injection
  in the Illinois Basin", *JGR: Solid Earth*, 2022. DOI 10.1029/2021JB023496.
  - Preprint: https://www.authorea.com/doi/full/10.1002/essoar.10508521.1
  - Nearly 20,000 microearthquakes were detected during about three years of injection,
    mostly in the basement below the reservoir.
  - Models seismicity rates on basement faults with Dieterich's rate-and-state model.
    Concludes the seismicity is mainly pressure-driven, with a poroelastic contribution.
- Seismological Society of America news item on an MIT-led study in *BSSA* (2024):
  https://www.seismosoc.org/news/pore-pressure-diffusion-led-to-microseismicity-at-illinois-basin-carbon-sequestration-site/
  - Covers the first injection period (late 2011–2014).
  - Attributes the 2011–2012 microearthquakes to pore-pressure diffusion along existing
    faults into the basement. Events were about Mw −2 to 1.
- Williams-Stroud et al., "Analysis of Microseismicity and Reactivated Fault Size to Assess
  the Potential for Felt Events by CO2 Injection in the Illinois Basin", *BSSA*, 2020:
  https://www.gwpc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Analysis-of-Microseismicity-and-Reactivated-Fault-Size-to-Assess-the-Potential-for-Felt-Events-by-CO2-Injection-in-the-Illinois-Basin.pdf
  - Notes that microseismicity has been detected only at the few sites injecting at
    commercial rates of about 1 Mt/yr or more.
- Bondarenko, Makhnenko & Podladchikov, *Science Advances*, as reported by Physics World:
  https://physicsworld.com/a/microscale-structure-of-rock-affects-microseismicity-at-underground-carbon-dioxide-storage-site/
  - Rock microstructure and stratigraphy shape the local microseismicity (events of
    magnitude 2.0 or less).
- "Evolution of induced microseismicity at the Illinois Basin – Decatur Project" (University
  of Illinois record):
  https://experts.illinois.edu/en/publications/evolution-of-induced-microseismicity-at-the-illinois-basin-decatu/
- Couëslan et al.: microseismic monitoring with geophone arrays in two wells found events
  in the Mt. Simon Sandstone, the pre-Mt. Simon unit and the Precambrian basement.
  (Reported by the search; no link was saved.)
- OSTI record 2426372: https://www.osti.gov/biblio/2426372

## Machine learning and catalogs (mostly DOE reports and posters)

- Chai & Maceira (ORNL), NETL 2023 Carbon Management meeting:
  https://netl.doe.gov/sites/default/files/netl-file/23CM_CTSp_Chai.pdf
  - A deep-learning event catalog from surface sensors: more than 8,000 events from about
    10 years of data at 17 stations. Intended to constrain fault and fracture locations.
- Lizama & Yoon (Sandia): CNN models that detect events in IBDP continuous waveforms,
  checked against the IBDP catalog. OSTI reports, e.g. https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/2483869
- Kumar, Harbert, Myshakin, Liu et al. (NETL SMART Phase II): unsupervised clustering of
  the IBDP microseismic catalog and injection data to map fracture networks.
  - https://netl.doe.gov/sites/default/files/netl-file/24CM/24CM_Posters_Harbert_0.pdf
  - A SMART slide gives event magnitudes as mostly below 0, with a maximum of about 1.5.
- CO2DataShare "Seismic Event Catalog" resource: the ISGS induced-microseismicity catalog
  dated 2021-06-28, Excel, about 9 MiB:
  https://co2datashare.org/dataset/62ccf48d-6d58-4c88-8abb-9e3ab6e5ca3d/resource/6eecec43-a77a-410c-a8c7-6fec1465d9b8
