  - The same issue has companion papers on the seismic and CO2 monitoring programme and

## Induced seismicity

- **Luu et al. (2022, *JGR: Solid Earth*):** DOI 10.1029/2021JB023496.
  - About 20,000 microearthquakes, mostly in the basement.
  - A rate-and-state model on basement faults explains them.
  - Mainly pressure-driven, with a poroelastic contribution.
- **MIT-led study (2024, *BSSA*):** the 2011–2012 events came from pore-pressure diffusion
  along existing faults into the basement. Events were about Mw −2 to 1.
- **Williams-Stroud et al. (2020, *BSSA*):** whether injection could cause felt events,
  judged from microseismicity and the size of reactivated faults.
- **Bondarenko, Makhnenko & Podladchikov (*Science Advances*):** rock microstructure and
  stratigraphy shape the microseismicity.
- **Couëslan et al.:** borehole geophone arrays in two wells found events in the
  Mt. Simon Sandstone, the pre-Mt. Simon unit and the Precambrian basement.
- **Dando et al. (2021, *Geophysical Journal International* 227(2): 1094–1122):**
  doi:10.1093/GJI/GGAB255. A modified double-difference relocation of the downhole-monitored
  events. The catalog's relocated subset comes from this paper. Source:
  [raw/2026-11-04-ibdp-injection-notes.md](../raw/2026-11-04-ibdp-injection-notes.md).

**Where they differ:** Luu et al. stress pressure with a poroelastic part. The MIT-led
study stresses pore-pressure diffusion along faults. Both tie the events to pressure from
injection reaching basement faults.

## Machine learning and event catalogs

These are mostly DOE reports and posters rather than journal papers.

- **Chai & Maceira (ORNL):** a deep-learning catalog of more than 8,000 events from about
  10 years of data at 17 surface stations.
- **Lizama & Yoon (Sandia):** CNN event detection in continuous IBDP waveforms, checked
  against the ISGS catalog.
- **Kumar, Harbert, Myshakin, Liu et al. (NETL SMART):** unsupervised clustering of the
  catalog with injection data to map fracture networks. Magnitudes are mostly below 0,
  with a maximum of about 1.5.

**Gap:** the search found no paper that uses the CO2DataShare release itself for machine
learning.
